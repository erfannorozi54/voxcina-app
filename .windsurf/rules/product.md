# Voxcina Android App

## Project Goal
Native Android client for the Voxcina e-commerce platform—a Persian online shopping experience with AI-powered features including smart search, conversational chatbot, and personalized product recommendations.

## Target Audience
Persian-speaking online shoppers seeking a modern, intuitive mobile shopping experience with intelligent product discovery and seamless checkout via Zibal payment gateway.

## Primary Value Proposition
- AI-powered Persian product search and recommendations
- Conversational shopping assistant for personalized guidance
- Hybrid cart syncing between anonymous and authenticated sessions
- Secure OTP authentication and integrated Zibal payments
- Real-time order tracking and wishlist management
